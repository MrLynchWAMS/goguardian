Rule Exceptions

A rule recategorizes any entry whose URL, title or search text contains the match text, optionally only within a date range (e.g. YouTube approved on the day of a lesson). Later rules override earlier ones. Everything re-categorizes instantly; stored data is not changed.

Default Detection Terms

Comma-separated, case-insensitive, matched anywhere in the URL/title. Searches containing an Academic term (or no term at all) are "Academic Search"; Distraction terms make them "Distraction Search".
